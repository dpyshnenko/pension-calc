I.

The accumulation — what grows

Every euro you put on the pensioenrekening is deductible from box 1 income up to your jaarruimte. The Belastingdienst refunds your contribution according to the three working-age brackets, and the model reinvests that refund along with your monthly deposit.

II.

The payout — what arrives monthly

At AOW age the pot converts into a fixed-duration annuity. The remaining balance keeps earning a (conservative) return as the bank pays you a level monthly amount until the pot reaches zero. On top of that sits the AOW state pension — and the combined gross is taxed at the lower AOW-age box 1 brackets.

A note on the math.

Section I assumes Factor A = 0 (no employer pension accrual), constant real income, and the 2026 jaarruimte formula: 30% × (income − €19,172), capped at €137,800 income. Deductions follow the working-age 3-tier box 1 schedule (35.75% / 37.56% / 49.50%). DEGIRO's pensioenrekening fee of 0.20%/year is deducted monthly from the balance.


Section II uses an ordinary annuity formula — pot × i / (1 − (1+i)−n) — assuming the remaining balance earns the chosen return during the payout phase. AOW gross is €1,637.57/month single or €1,122.12/month per partner, plus vakantiegeld accrued at €106.55 (single) or €76.10 (couple) per month. Tax follows the 2026 AOW-age 3-tier box 1 brackets (17.85% / 37.56% / 49.50%) but excludes heffingskortingen, which typically reduce the total annual tax bill further.


Verify your actual jaarruimte at belastingdienst.nl, your projected AOW age at svb.nl, and your real pension accrual at mijnpensioenoverzicht.nl.
